Spectroscopist Handbook

Spectroscopy calculations and ideas as web pages: overlap factors, beam alignment and spectrum processing, moved from Igor Pro into the browser so they are easy to use. Every page is kept here as a copy, so the handbook is one site. Click a preview or a title to open a page; "source" opens the page's own repository and "also at" its standalone site.

Pages

Franck–Condon overlap factors

Two harmonic potential energy surfaces. Set ωg, ωe, the displacement and the starting level v″; the page gives every overlap ⟨χ′|χ″⟩, the Franck–Condon factors and the vibronic stick spectrum, with the closed form for each row.

Notation from McHale, Molecular Spectroscopy. source

Gaussian beam focal overlap

Pump and probe Gaussian beams crossing in a sample. The intensity-weighted overlap fv and the absolute overlap η for 2PA, transient absorption and 2P-excited TA, with the crossing plane, the beam cross-section, an overlap map and delay and position scans.

source · also at beamoverlap.koemel.net

Find the overlap (game)

Steer the pump with a kinematic mirror and slide the delay stage until the pulses meet in the sample. The cyclohexane Raman spectrum grows as you get closer; hold 95 % overlap to lock in. Three beams per game and three levels; hard has 50 fs pulses and small beams.

source · also at beamoverlap.game.koemel.net. Best times are kept per site, in your browser.

Beam overlap screensaver

A pump and a probe spot drift around a black screen and bounce off the edges, like the old bouncing-logo screensaver. Where they cross, the signal glows white and the corner shows the overlap. Each load picks new colours, waists and pulse lengths; add a seed to the address (#48213, #classic) to keep one. Double-click for full screen.

Also at screensaver.overlap.koemel.net

Solvent subtraction

Removes solvent Raman bands from a spectrum, written for FSRS data. Load Igor Pro waves (.ibw, .itx, .pxp) or text, fit the solvent by a scale factor, a Taylor expansion (shift and broadening) or an exact shift-and-broadening model, and download the result as an Igor wave.

source

Lorentz oscillator: absorption and refractive index

α(ω) and n(ω) of a medium of up to three Lorentz oscillators, solved exactly from ñ = √(nbg² + χ̃), with the dilute and near-resonance Lorentzian approximations to compare. Examples: Na vapour, He⁺, air, and fused silica and BK7 glass built from their Sellmeier terms.

Glass data from Malitson (1965) and the Schott N-BK7 data sheet.

Tools

Exam scan check

Compares every page of a scanned stack of exams with a blank copy and lists missing, duplicated, out-of-order, upside-down and blank pages. Then turn the pages that need it and save a corrected PDF, without recompressing the scan. The PDFs stay in the browser: the page is locked so it cannot send them anywhere.

PDF reading with pdf.js, bundled with the page.

Notes

  • The calculations run in your browser. Nothing you type or load is uploaded.
  • Each page is a static folder in this repository (franck-condon/, focal-overlap/, overlap-game/, screensaver/, solvent-subtraction/, lorentz-explorer/, exam-scan/): open its index.html and it runs with no build step. The exam scan page has to be served, by this site or any local web server, because browsers do not load its PDF reader straight from disk. The calculation pages list their model and equations at the bottom.
  • The Franck–Condon, solvent subtraction, Lorentz oscillator and exam scan pages come with their tests (node franck-condon/tests/validate.mjs), which check the calculation against closed forms, numerical integration and published values; their READMEs give the numbers.
